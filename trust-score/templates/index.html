What We Do

We verify the authenticity of e-commerce product reviews using advanced AI and Natural Language Processing. Our system scrapes, analyzes, and classifies every review to help you distinguish genuine feedback from fake, paid, or misleading reviews — so you can shop with confidence.

We currently cover India's Top 3 E-Commerce Platforms, helping millions of shoppers make smarter, safer purchasing decisions every day.

Our Mission

Online reviews heavily influence purchasing decisions, but a significant percentage of reviews on e-commerce platforms are fake, misleading, or paid. Our mission is to empower consumers with AI-driven tools to identify deceptive reviews and make informed purchasing decisions.

This project uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) combined with behavioral analysis to classify reviews as genuine or fake with high accuracy.
