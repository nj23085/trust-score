About This Project

An AI-powered system that detects fake and deceptive product reviews across major Indian e-commerce platforms using advanced machine learning techniques.

Our Mission

Online reviews heavily influence purchasing decisions, but a significant percentage of reviews on e-commerce platforms are fake, misleading, or paid. Our mission is to empower consumers with AI-driven tools to identify deceptive reviews and make informed purchasing decisions.

This project uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) combined with behavioral analysis to classify reviews as genuine or fake with high accuracy.

How It Works

1

Paste Product URL

Copy any product URL from Amazon, Flipkart, or Myntra and paste it into our analyzer.

2

AI Scrapes & Analyzes

Our system scrapes reviews and runs them through a trained ML model that examines text patterns, behavior signals, and linguistic features.

3

Get Detailed Results

View a comprehensive dashboard with trust scores, charts, keyword analysis, and individual review classifications.
