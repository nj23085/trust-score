Information We Collect

We collect only the product URL voluntarily provided by the user and extract limited information, including the product identifier (ASIN) and country code, strictly for the purpose of conducting our analysis.

Information We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information of customers.
  • We do not collect or process customers' financial information.
  • We do not track or store customers' browsing history.
  • We do not collect or maintain passwords or login credentials, as no account creation is required.

Data Retention & Analysis Results

We save the outcomes of our AI analysis, such as the authenticity score, percentage of fake reviews, adjusted rating, explanations, and examples of flagged reviews. This enables us to provide users with a shareable permanent link for analysing the same product again, making the process faster and more efficient.

We do not store IP addresses in our database or link them with analyses.

Chrome Extension Permissions

The Trust-Score Chrome Extension requires the following permissions to function:

  • Active Tab: To read product page content and extract review data from the currently active tab.
  • Storage: To save user preferences and cached analysis results locally on your device.
  • Host Permissions: To access product pages on Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra for review scraping.

The extension operates entirely on the client side for data extraction. Scraped reviews are sent only to the Trust-Score server for ML analysis and are not shared with any third parties.
